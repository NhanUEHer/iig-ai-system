Nội dung gốc

Dear Ms. Joseph,
I am writing in reference to the partnership proposal that you sent to HF Manufacturers on 8 March. I am pleased to inform you that our Vice President, Mr. Howard, would like to meet with representatives from your company to discuss the proposal in more detail.
Mr. Howard will be away on business until 18 March, so he suggests holding the meeting on 20 March at 10:00 A.M. in our head office. If this date does not suit you, please let us know two or three alternative dates and we will do our best to accommodate them.
Because this meeting is crucial to our first cooperation, our finance advisor, Ms. Lee, will also attend. We would appreciate it if you could bring the cost estimates and the delivery schedule mentioned in your proposal…

proposal

NOUNHoàn tất
/prəˈpəʊzl/·trong bài: “proposal” · câu 01
1Dạng trong bài proposal
2Từ (dạng từ điển) proposal
3Loại từ Noun
4IPA /prəˈpəʊzl/
5Nghĩa tiếng Việt bản đề xuất
6Định nghĩa tiếng Anh A formal plan or suggestion put forward for consideration.
7Câu gốc I am writing in reference to the partnership proposal that you sent to HF Manufacturers on 8 March.
8Ngữ cảnh “proposal” chỉ bản đề xuất hợp tác mà bà Joseph đã gửi cho công ty.
9Ví dụ The board approved the proposal for a new branch office.
10Dịch ví dụ Hội đồng quản trị đã phê duyệt đề xuất mở văn phòng chi nhánh mới.
11Collocations submit a proposal, business proposal
12Đồng nghĩa plan, suggestion, offer
13Word family propose (v), proposal (n)
