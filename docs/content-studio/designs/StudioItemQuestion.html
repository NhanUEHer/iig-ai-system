Nội dung gốc

Dear Ms. Joseph,

I am writing in reference to the partnership proposal that you sent to HF Manufacturers on 8 March. I am pleased to inform you that our Vice President, Mr. Howard, would like to meet with representatives from your company to discuss the proposal in more detail.

Mr. Howard will be away on business until 18 March, so he suggests holding the meeting on 20 March at 10:00 A.M. in our head office. If this date does not suit you, please let us know two or three alternative dates…

Because this meeting is crucial to our first cooperation, our finance advisor, Ms. Lee, will also attend…

Please confirm your attendance … before 15 March.
